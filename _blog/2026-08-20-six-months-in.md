I started with two-hour Sunday sessions and a spreadsheet I was proud of. Six weeks later the spreadsheet was immaculate and my listening had not moved.

What changed things was twenty minutes a day, always at the same time, always with a timer. The sessions are worse individually and much better in aggregate.

The other change was writing things down publicly. Knowing a drill would end up on this page made me finish it properly.

## The parallels

This felt obvious in hindsight. I write code the same way — consistent daily work beats midnight scrambles. The same decision-making applies: finish small, finish often, ship without apology.

Studying French is easier than shipping software, but the rhythms are identical.
